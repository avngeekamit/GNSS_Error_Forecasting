# References

This directory documents the research papers and technical resources
consulted during the development of the GNSS error forecasting
framework.

The references provide background and supporting research related to:

- GNSS satellite clock estimation
- GNSS ephemeris and orbit information
- Satellite error modelling
- GNSS time-series forecasting
- Machine learning for GNSS applications
- Multi-GNSS and real-time satellite clock estimation

---

## Research References

**Affiliations:**

National Time Service Center, Chinese Academy of Sciences  
Key Laboratory of Time Reference and Applications, Chinese Academy of Sciences  
University of Chinese Academy of Sciences

Chang'an University

**Relevance:**

This work is relevant to the satellite clock-error component of this
research and provides background on real-time satellite clock
estimation using GNSS observations and additional observation sources.

---

## How References Are Used

The references provide background and research context for GNSS error
modelling, satellite clock estimation, and forecasting.

They are not treated as direct validation of the models developed in
this repository.

The experimental results reported in this repository are generated
from the datasets and forecasting models contained within the project.
